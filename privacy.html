Privacy Policy

Platform: ugcord (a self-hosted Fluxer instance)
Operator: ugdc (operated by @alecks)
Contact: me@alecks.dev
Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information ugcord collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and what rights you have. ugcord is a small, privately run community server operated by ugdc (“we”, “us”).

1. Who runs this instance

ugcord is self-hosted and operated by ugdc, which is run by @alecks. It runs on privately owned hardware and infrastructure in Perth, Western Australia. It is not hosted on a third-party cloud provider, and no other person or organization has administrative access to the server or its data. The one exception is Cloudflare, which handles traffic in transit (see section 5).

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Username and display name
  • Email address (used for sign-in, verification and account recovery)
  • Password, stored only as a salted hash (we cannot see your actual password)
  • Profile details you choose to add, such as an avatar, banner or bio

Content you create

  • Messages, including direct messages and group messages
  • Files, images and other attachments you upload
  • Reactions, edits, and other activity within the app
  • Server, channel and role settings you create

Technical information

  • IP addresses and timestamps associated with logins and requests
  • Device or browser information (such as user agent) sent when you connect
  • Session tokens that keep you signed in
  • Server logs used to diagnose problems and prevent abuse

Voice and video

Real-time voice and video are transmitted between participants and are not recorded or stored by this instance.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the service (authentication, delivering messages, storing uploads)
  • To keep the instance secure and prevent spam, abuse and unauthorized access
  • To investigate reports and moderate content where necessary
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to the service
  • To troubleshoot and maintain the server

Your information is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics sold to others, or training AI models.

4. Who can access your data

Only the operator (@alecks) has access to the server and its data. We will access message content, files or account data only for moderation and safety purposes, such as reviewing a report of abuse, illegal content or a rule violation, or resolving a technical problem affecting your account. We do not browse private conversations out of curiosity.

Messages are stored on the server so they can sync between your devices. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so they are technically readable by anyone with access to the backend database. That access is limited to the operator and used only as described above.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We will only disclose information if:

  • required to do so by Australian law, a court order or a valid legal request;
  • necessary to protect the safety of a person, or to report suspected child exploitation or other serious criminal activity to the appropriate authorities; or
  • you ask us to (for example, a data export).

Third parties: This instance does not use third-party analytics or advertising services. Some features may involve third parties you or other users interact with, for example if a user posts a link or embed to an external site, your browser may contact that site when it loads.

Cloudflare: We use Cloudflare as a reverse proxy for DDoS protection and network transit. This means traffic, including IP addresses and message content in transit, passes through Cloudflare’s systems and is subject to Cloudflare’s privacy policy. We don’t control how Cloudflare handles that data.

6. Data storage, location and security

Data is stored on hardware physically located in Perth, Western Australia. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including backups, Cloudflare (DDoS protection, network transit), strict firewalls and access controls, VLAN segmentation and SSH login alerts.

Backups: We keep local and offsite backups. Offsite backups are stored at another privately owned location, not on public cloud.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify affected users as soon as reasonably possible.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account information and content: kept while your account is active.
  • Deleted messages and files: removed from the live system when you delete them, and from backups when those backups rotate out (every 7–14 days).
  • Server logs (including IP addresses): kept for approximately 30 days.
  • Deleted accounts: your account data is deleted within 30 days of a deletion request, except where we must retain limited information to deal with an ongoing abuse or legal matter.

8. Your rights and choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • give you a copy of it;
  • correct anything that is inaccurate;
  • delete your account and associated personal information.

To make a request, email me@alecks.dev. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will aim to respond within 30 days.

Under Australian law (the Privacy Act 1988), many small operators are exempt from the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy is provided voluntarily as a commitment to handle your information responsibly, and we intend to follow its spirit regardless. If you are located elsewhere (for example, in the European Union or United Kingdom), you may have additional rights under your local law, and you can contact us to exercise them.

9. Children

This instance is intended for users who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age. If you believe a younger person has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it.

10. Cookies and local storage

This instance uses only what is needed for the service to work, such as session tokens and settings stored in your browser or app. It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do, and we will let users know of significant changes through an announcement on the instance.
